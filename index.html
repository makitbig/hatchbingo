FAQ

How do I play?

Tap a tile to grow it and the tiles above, below, left and right by one stage: egg → cracking egg → chick → egg again. Fill three lines (rows, columns or diagonals) with chicks and the big egg hatches into a real bird. From level 25 a cuckoo hides among the eggs: its tile stops changing, and a line through it can't be completed.

Where do my birds go?

Every bird you hatch goes into the Bird Book and grows up in the Aviary in real time, so come back the next day to see how it has grown.

How do I get coins and items?

Coins come from winning boards and from the Daily Egg. Spend them on Hint, Undo and +3 taps, or watch an optional video for one. Coins and items can't be bought with money.

What is in Hatch Bingo Pro?

A one-time purchase that removes the banner ad, lets you use Hint, Undo and +3 taps without videos, and opens the Tropics, Rare birds, Lost birds and Dinosaur kin packs (16 more birds, 80 new boards).

I bought Pro on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the menu and tap Restore purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your birds and progress are saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Who made the game?

Hatch Bingo was designed by Eugene Kim, who also drew the eggs and chicks and made the music, and built by Blucos. The bird pictures were made with AI (Gemini) and checked one by one against the real birds' colors and markings.
